NOCT Publisher

NOCT Publisher is the tool we use to post NOCT's own videos to NOCT's own TikTok account, @noct.88, through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

What it does

Our render pipeline produces each NOCT short as a finished video file. The Publisher lists those files, and we pick one, set its title and who can see it, and post it to our TikTok account or save it to our TikTok drafts.

Who uses it

Only the NOCT team, on the NOCT TikTok account. There is no sign-up and no public access. It never connects to, reads from, or posts for any other TikTok user.

What it never does

It does not read followers, comments, messages or analytics, does not collect any third-party user's data, and does not post anything we did not make ourselves.

Permissions

TikTok scopes requested

ScopeWhy we need it
user.info.basicShows the connected account's display name and avatar, so we can confirm it is @noct.88 before posting.
video.uploadSends one of our own videos to the account's TikTok drafts to finish in the TikTok app.
video.publishPosts one of our own videos directly to the account with the chosen title and privacy level.

Tokens are stored server-side only and deleted on disconnection or on request. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Flow

How a post is made

  1. Connect

    We sign in to the NOCT TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit and approve the three scopes.

  2. Choose

    We pick a rendered NOCT video, write the caption and choose a privacy level from the options TikTok allows for the account.

  3. Post

    The video is sent through the Content Posting API, and the Publisher shows TikTok's processing status until the post is live.

The tool

Publisher console

The console talks to the Publisher service running on NOCT's own machine, so it only works there; anywhere else it will show that the service is not running. Watch a recording of it in use.
