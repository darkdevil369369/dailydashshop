NOCT Publisher

NOCT Publisher is the tool we use to post DECODED AI's own videos to DECODED AI's own TikTok account, @decoded_ai, through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

What it does

Our render pipeline produces each DECODED AI Short as a finished video file. The Publisher lists those files, and we pick one, set its title and who can see it, and post it to our TikTok account or save it to our TikTok drafts.

Who uses it

Only the DECODED AI team, on the DECODED AI TikTok account. There is no sign-up and no public access. It never connects to, reads from, or posts for any other TikTok user.

What it never does

It does not read followers, comments, messages or analytics, does not collect any third-party user's data, and does not post anything we did not make ourselves.

Permissions

TikTok scopes requested

ScopeWhy we need it
user.info.basicShows the connected account's display name and avatar, so we can confirm it is @decoded_ai before posting.
video.uploadSends one of our own videos to the account's TikTok drafts to finish in the TikTok app.
video.publishPosts one of our own videos directly to the account with the chosen title and privacy level.

Tokens are stored server-side only and deleted on disconnection or on request. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Flow

How a post is made

  1. Connect

    We sign in to the DECODED AI TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit and approve the three scopes.

  2. Choose

    We pick a rendered DECODED AI video and preview it. The form loads the account's current TikTok settings every time: we edit the caption, pick who can view it (no default), choose whether to allow comments, duets and stitches, and disclose any commercial content.

  3. Post

    Nothing is sent until we press Post and accept TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation. The video then goes through the Content Posting API, and the Publisher shows TikTok's processing status until the post is live.

The tool

Publisher console

The console talks to the Publisher service running on our own machine, so it only works there; anywhere else it will show that the service is not running. Nothing is sent to TikTok until you press Post.
