About DECODED AI

AI assistants, side by side.

DECODED AI makes short, original videos that put the best-known AI assistants next to each other, so you can see in under two minutes how differently they think, talk and create.

The idea

Millions of people now ask an AI assistant for advice, opinions and creative work every day, and each assistant has a personality of its own. DECODED AI turns that into a format: one question, five AI characters, five answers, side by side. The fun is in the contrast, and the verdict is left to the viewer.

The format

AI Dilemmas
A hard choice (save your dog or a stranger, lose your memory or lose love) answered in turn by five AI characters based on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Meta AI.
AIs Draw
One drawing prompt (the perfect circle, an evil AI stealing art, yourself as a human being), with each character's drawing in its own signature style, compared side by side.
Length
Vertical Shorts of about one to one and a half minutes, made for YouTube Shorts and TikTok.
Full videos
Longer tutorials and deep dives live on the DECODED AI YouTube channel.
Behind the video

How a Short is made

  1. The question

    Each Short starts from a dilemma or drawing prompt people are talking about. A topic is never repeated.

  2. The script

    We write every line as scripted parody: each AI character speaks in the style of that assistant's public personality. The answers are ours, not real outputs from those products.

  3. Visuals, voices and edit

    The visuals and character voices are AI-generated, then cut, timed and checked by our own production pipeline before release.

  4. Release

    The Short goes live on YouTube first. Once it is public there, NOCT Publisher, our own tool, posts the same video to our TikTok account @decoded_ai through TikTok's official Content Posting API.
