One prompt, five AIs

Every video puts a single question or drawing prompt to five AI characters, each written to match the public personality of a well-known assistant, so the contrast between them is the whole story.

Two series

AI Dilemmas: save your dog or a stranger, lose your memory or lose love. AIs Draw: the perfect circle, an evil AI stealing art, the demon under the bed.

Original and labelled

Every video is scripted and edited by us. The AI answers are written by us as parody, not real product outputs, and the visuals and voices are AI-generated. Nothing is re-uploaded from other creators.

How releases work

YouTube first, then TikTok

On YouTube

New Shorts go live several times a week on the DECODED AI YouTube channel, alongside longer tutorials and deep dives.

On TikTok

Once a Short is public on YouTube, our own tool, NOCT Publisher, posts the same video to our TikTok account @decoded_ai through TikTok's official Content Posting API.
