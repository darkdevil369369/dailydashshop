One question per episode

Why unfinished tasks stick in your head. Why nobody slept through the night before 1800. Each episode takes one question and answers it properly in about nine minutes.

Checked against the research

Every claim is traced to its source: the original study, the replications, and the papers that disagree. When a famous effect fails to replicate, that becomes the story.

Drawn, not stock

No stock footage and no borrowed clips. Every beat of the script gets its own drawing in one consistent stick-figure style, so an idea is something you can see.
