NOCT Publisher

NOCT is a hand-drawn explainer channel: every episode starts with a fact that argues with itself, then pulls the contradiction apart in plain English, with a drawing for every beat. New episodes land twice a week, and short cuts land on the days in between.

What this app does

NOCT Publisher is our own internal publishing tool. It takes videos we produced ourselves and uploads them to our own NOCT social accounts on a schedule, so a person does not have to be awake at the publish slot.

  • Reads a video file that our render pipeline has already produced.
  • Signs in to our own NOCT account with that platform's official login, with our permission.
  • Uploads the video with its title and description at the scheduled time.

Who can use it

Nobody but us. It is not a product, there is no sign-up, and it never publishes on behalf of any account other than the NOCT accounts we own.

What it never does

  • It does not read, collect, or store anyone else's content or personal data.
  • It does not post third-party or re-uploaded content — every frame and the voice are ours.
  • It does not buy, sell, or share any data with anyone.
