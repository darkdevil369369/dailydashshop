Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy covers the NOCT website at dailydashshop.com/noct and NOCT Publisher ("the app"), the tool we use to post NOCT's own videos to NOCT's own TikTok account. Both are operated by Daily Dash Shop ("we", "us"), Bhalawani, Pandharpur, Solapur, Maharashtra 413310, India.

1. The website

The website has no accounts, no sign-up, no comments and no forms. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Episode thumbnails are loaded from YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com), and links to YouTube and TikTok take you to those services, whose own privacy policies apply once you are there. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operation.

2. Who the app is for

NOCT Publisher is used only by us, to publish videos we made ourselves to the TikTok account we own (@noct.88). It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and never connects to or acts on behalf of any other TikTok user. It does not access any third party's TikTok data.

3. TikTok data the app accesses

When we connect our own TikTok account through TikTok's official Login Kit, we grant the app these scopes, and use them only as described:

  • user.info.basic: the connected account's open ID, display name and avatar. Used only to show which account is connected, so we can confirm it is the NOCT account before posting.
  • video.upload: to send one of our own video files to the account's TikTok drafts.
  • video.publish: to publish one of our own videos directly to the account, with the title and privacy level we choose.

The app also reads the creator posting options TikTok returns (for example, the allowed privacy levels) and the processing status of each post it makes. It does not read followers, viewers, comments, messages, likes, analytics, or any other user's profile or content.

4. How TikTok data is stored

The access token and refresh token TikTok issues are stored server-side, in a private file on our own server that only the service account running the app can read. Tokens are never sent to the browser, never embedded in this website, never written to any public location, and never committed to a code repository. The one-time authorisation code TikTok returns after login is exchanged for tokens on the server and is not kept.

5. How TikTok data is used

Solely to post NOCT's own videos to NOCT's own TikTok account and to confirm those posts completed. We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or training any model, and we do not combine it with other data.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share TikTok data or any other personal data with anyone. The only network calls the app makes with it are to TikTok's own official API.

7. Retention

Tokens and the cached profile (open ID, display name, avatar URL) are kept only while the app is connected to the NOCT account. Access tokens expire after 24 hours and are refreshed only while the integration is in use. When the app is disconnected, or on request, the stored tokens and cached profile are deleted from our server. The app keeps no copy of TikTok data beyond that one private token file; the IDs and status of our own posts are held only for the few minutes it takes to confirm each post completed.

8. Deletion and revocation

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's app settings (Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions), which immediately ends the app's access. You can also email noctofficial08@gmail.com to ask us to delete any data we hold; we delete it from our server within 30 days of the request, and normally much sooner, and confirm by email.

9. Security

All calls to TikTok use HTTPS. The app's local control service listens only on the server itself and is not reachable from the internet. The app's client secret is kept server-side alongside the tokens.

10. Children

Neither the website nor the app is directed at children under 13, and neither collects data from them.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

12. Contact
