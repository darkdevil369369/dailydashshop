Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

NOCT Publisher ("the app") is an internal publishing tool operated by Daily Dash Shop ("we", "us"). It uploads videos we produced ourselves to social accounts we own. It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-up.

Information we collect

The app collects no personal information from anyone other than the account owner who authorises it — that is us. When we connect a social account, the platform gives the app:

  • An access token and refresh token for that account.
  • Basic profile information for that account (display name, avatar, open ID), used only to confirm the app is connected to the correct account.

We do not collect data about viewers, followers, commenters, or any other person. We do not use cookies, analytics, or trackers on this site.

How we use it

Tokens are used for exactly one purpose: to upload our own videos to our own accounts at the scheduled time. Nothing else is done with them.

How we store it

Tokens are stored in a private file on our own server, readable only by the account that runs the app. They are never written to a public location and never appear in the app's public repository.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share this data with anyone. It is not sent to any third party, advertiser, or data broker. The only network calls the app makes are to the official APIs of the platforms we publish to.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the integration is in use. We can revoke access at any time from the platform's own app-permissions settings, and doing so immediately ends the app's access. On revocation or on request, the stored tokens are deleted from our server.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
